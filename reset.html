Device reset

Reset your devices

A Switchboard Pro license activates up to 3 Macs. If you still have a Mac you no longer use, the cleanest way to free its slot is in the app — Settings → License & Access → Deactivate. Use this page only when you can't get to that Mac (you sold, lost, or wiped it): it frees all your slots so you can re-activate on the Mac you're using now.
