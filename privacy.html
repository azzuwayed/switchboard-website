Privacy & trust

Your app data stays on your Mac.

Switchboard is a local-first macOS app. Your services, logs, and settings stay on your Mac. This page explains the limited information we collect and how we use it.

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

Summary

  • Your configuration, logs, and docs stay on your Mac.
  • We collect limited usage data to count active installs and improve the app.
  • Free use needs no account.
  • We never sell your information.

Information we collect

Usage data. Switchboard sends us limited usage data, such as app version, device and system information, approximate location, and how the app is used. If you sign in, this data may be linked to your account. It never includes your files, services, or commands.

Account and purchases. If you sign in, subscribe, or buy a license, we process the information needed to provide that service, such as your name, email address, purchase and license details, and the devices registered to your access.

Support. When you contact support, we receive what you send us and basic app and system details. Logs and diagnostics are included only if you choose to attach them.

How we use it

We use this information to provide and secure Switchboard, deliver and verify purchases, answer support requests, and understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Switchboard, such as hosting, payment, and email providers, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Usage data is kept for up to 13 months; statistics that do not identify you may be kept longer. Account and purchase records are kept while your account is active and as required by law.

What stays on your Mac

Your services, logs, docs, approvals, and settings are stored locally and are not synced to your account. Switchboard does not scan your files and does not request Full Disk Access, Accessibility, or Screen Recording.

Security

Switchboard has no inbound network listener. The interface cannot run arbitrary shell commands, risky actions require your confirmation, and app updates are signed and verified before they install.

Your choices

You can use Switchboard without an account, and you can sign out at any time. To ask about or delete information linked to your account, contact us.

Changes and contact

We update this page when our practices change. Questions? Use the contact form or account support.
